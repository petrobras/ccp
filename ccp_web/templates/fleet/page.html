Fleet analysis

Compressors built to one design differ by manufacturing tolerances, installation, instruments and degradation. Their field evaluations and shop tests are pooled in one Bayesian model, so each unit is judged against what is normal for its design and a deviation shared by the whole fleet (an optimistic vendor curve, an equation-of-state bias) is not taken for a problem of one unit.
